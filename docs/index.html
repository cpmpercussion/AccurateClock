Accurate Clock

An iOS & iPadOS clock that does the one thing iOS’s built-in Clock app won’t: shows you the seconds, clearly. Built for setting mechanical watches that hack.

  • Sweep or tick second hand
  • Synced against time.apple.com via SNTP
  • 48-city world time picker (the Casio AE-1200 set)
  • No accounts. No tracking. No analytics. Privacy policy.

By Charles Martin. Source: github.com/cpmpercussion/AccurateClock.
